Paint Color Matcher

Saw a house color you love? Sample it from a photo or type in a hex code, and find the closest Sherwin-Williams, Behr, and Benjamin Moore matches to take to the paint store.

These are ballpark matches, not a guaranteed formula. The hex values behind each swatch are approximate, publicly-circulated references we compiled for this tool — not official data from Sherwin-Williams, Behr, or Benjamin Moore. Phone cameras, screens, and lighting all shift color too, so treat the results below as a shopping-list starting point. The only way to get an exact formula is to bring an actual sample (or the summary this tool generates) into the store and have them scan or eyeball it against their own chips.

3. Combonations

A starting point, not gospel — trim and accent are generated from your body color (and each other) using basic color-wheel relationships, then matched back to real paint chips. Lock a row once you like it, or choose your own color for it directly — the rest keep suggesting around whatever you've fixed. Trust your eye over this.
