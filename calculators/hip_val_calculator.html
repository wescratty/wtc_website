Hip & Valley Sheathing Angle Calculator

Find the bevel angle to cut a sheet of roof sheathing so it lays flush along a hip or valley.

Hip / Valley Sheathing Angle

Enter the pitch of each roof plane that meets at the hip or valley (they don't need to match). This gives you the bevel angle to trim each plane's sheathing so the cut edge lays flush along that line, plus a way to mark it on a real sheet without a protractor.

/ 12
/ 12

Tip: for a standard hip or valley (a square 90° corner in plan), enter the same pitch on both sides if the two roof sections match, or different pitches for an irregular hip/valley.
