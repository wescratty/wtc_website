Home Building Process, Step by Step

How a custom home comes together, from design and permitting through rough-in, finish trades, and final inspection.

PRE-CONSTRUCTION
Design
Concept & layout — often iterates with budget
Drawings
Construction documents used to build, price & permit the job
Budget
Finalized estimate — may send design back for revision if over
Permitting
Survey & zoning setbacks review, permit application & approval — required if within city limits; requirements vary in unincorporated areas
Site Prep
Relocate gas, electrical & other utility lines out of the dig zone
Excavation & Foundation
Framing
Dry-In
Sheathing, house wrap & windows/doors — walls weathertight, but roof deck stays bare for now
ROUGH-IN — least flexible material first
1. Plumbing
Drain lines need gravity slope — hardest to reroute later
2. HVAC Ductwork
Bulky — needs straight, direct runs
3. Electrical
Flexible wire — easiest to route around the other two trades
⚠ Common mistake Electrical rough-in before HVAC forces HVAC to cut wires out of duct paths.
Roofing
Shingles/membrane go on now — flashing is sealed around penetrations that already exist, instead of guessing early or cutting finished roofing later
Insulation
Drywall
FINISH TRADES — same order as rough-in
Plumbing Finish
Fixtures, faucets
HVAC Finish
Registers, thermostat
Electrical Finish
Outlets, switches, fixtures
Flooring
Paint & Trim
Final Inspection
Remodel
...and it starts over 🔁
