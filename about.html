Serving the Bitterroot Valley for 30+ Years

Locally owned and operated, WTC Montana Construction has been building and remodeling homes throughout the Bitterroot Valley for over three decades.

Wesley Cratty, Founder

WTC Montana Construction is a hands-on, detail-driven general contracting business built around 30+ years of fieldwork in the Bitterroot Valley. Founder Wesley Cratty personally handles drafting and design on custom work — when a client wanted a hand-drawn three-centered arch roofline for a porch rebuild, Wes worked out the geometry by hand and built it into the plans himself, rather than pulling a stock design off the shelf. That's the standard behind every project, from a small repair to a full custom home.

We take on the full range of residential work: drafting & design, new construction, remodels & additions, maintenance and handyman work, masonry and chimney rebuilds, and foundations & flatwork — serving homeowners from Hamilton to Stevensville, south to Darby, MT (Bitterroot Valley).
