Tile Work

Bitterroot Valley, MT · Bathroom & Kitchen Tile

← Back to Portfolio

Two separate tile projects in this gallery. The first is a full bathroom remodel — demo down to the studs, new cement board, a waterproofing membrane, hex tile flooring, a mosaic accent panel, and subway tile walls, finished top to bottom. The second is a kitchen floor upgrade: the old tile came out, a new underlayment membrane went down, and new tile was set and laid with careful attention to level using a laser — the kind of detail that keeps a large tile floor looking right for the long run.

Whether it's a full bathroom gut or a straightforward floor upgrade, tile work lives or dies on prep — waterproofing, substrate, and layout — done right before a single tile goes down.
